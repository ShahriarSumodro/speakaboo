### Full Description (English)

```markdown
Welcome to Speakaboo — the AI English speaking companion built to help learners of all ages build genuine spoken fluency, natural pronunciation, and conversational confidence!

Whether you are a child starting your English journey, a school student practicing for exams, or a university student preparing for IELTS and job interviews, Speakaboo makes English speaking practice enjoyable, low-anxiety, and accessible every day.

🦉 WHY LEARNERS LOVE SPEAKABOO:

1. TALK TO REAL CHARACTERS WITH PERSONALITY
Choose your favorite companion:
• Farmer Reggie (Reginald Green): Patient, warm, and encouraging. Perfect for beginners and young learners!
• Detective Kabir (Kabir Chowdhury): Analytical, clever, and sharp. Solve fun mysteries and practice deductive reasoning!
• Professor Anika: Scholarly, articulate, and thoughtful. Perfect for IELTS speaking topics, environmental science, and academic discussions!

2. NATURAL RECASTS & GENTLE CORRECTIONS
Never fear making mistakes! Speakaboo's companions practice natural recasting — they repeat your idea using natural English inside their reply, giving explicit tips for only the single most important error so you stay confident and keep talking.

3. STRUCTURED DAILY HABIT (THE 2-MINUTE LOOP)
• Word of the Day: Learn curated vocabulary with clear Bengali meanings and pronunciation.
• Daily Missions & Streaks: Complete a 2-minute daily talk to maintain your streak and earn XP.
• Streak Forgiveness: Life gets busy! Use Streak Freezes or our zero-guilt recovery sessions so you never lose your hard-earned progress.

4. TARGETED PRACTICE MODES
• Real-World Roleplay Scenarios: Ordering food, job interviews, asking for directions, and doctor visits with interactive goal checklists.
• Guided Discussion Topics: Explore nature, space, hobbies, weather, and world affairs.
• Audio Shadowing: Listen to native speech with speed control (0.8x to 1.2x) and practice sentence rhythm.
• Leitner Vocabulary Notebook: Save your corrected words and master them with spaced-repetition flashcards.

5. DESIGNED FOR BANGLADESH
• Affordable Pro Access: Unlimited daily practice for only 200 BDT/month via bKash and Nagad.
• Try Before You Unlock: 2 minutes of free conversation with every premium character.
• Low Data Usage: Optimized audio streaming that runs smoothly even on 3G and budget Android phones.
• Warm Dark Theme: Practice comfortably at night without eye strain.

🔒 CHILD SAFETY & PRIVACY PROMISE
• ZERO RAW AUDIO STORAGE: Your voice is processed in-memory to generate the spoken reply and discarded immediately. We NEVER store raw audio recordings.
• Parent Dashboard: Gated behind a secure 4-digit Parent PIN. View weekly speaking minutes, words learned, and safety summaries.
• Safe Persona Redirects: Harmful or inappropriate topics are gently guided back to positive learning in character voice.
• Complete Data Portability & Deletion: Export your learning history or delete your entire account anytime with one tap.

Download Speakaboo today and start speaking English with confidence!
```

## 3. Google Play Data Safety Declarations

### A. Data Collection & Purpose
| Data Type | Collected? | Shared? | Purpose | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Voice / Raw Audio** | **NO** | **NO** | Streamed in-memory for STT transcription; strictly discarded immediately. | 0 seconds (Never stored). |
| **Pseudonymous Device ID** | Yes | No | Account identity, saving streaks, XP, level, and vocabulary cards. | Retained until user deletes account. |
| **User Profile (Name, Age Band, Goal)** | Yes | No | Personalizing AI companion difficulty and tone. | Retained until user updates or deletes account. |
| **App Performance & Crash Logs** | Yes | No | App stability, monitoring, and error diagnostics. | 30 days rolling. |
| **Financial / Payment Records** | Yes | No | Verifying bKash/Nagad transactions and active entitlements. | Retained for accounting/legal compliance. |

### B. Security Practices
- **Data Encrypted in Transit:** Yes (All communication uses TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
- **Data Deletion Request Mechanism:** Yes (Instant in-app account purge via `SettingsScreen` or API `DELETE /api/privacy/user/{user_id}`).
- **Data Not Sold:** No personal or usage data is ever sold or leased to third parties.

## 4. Families Policy & COPPA Compliance

1. **Targeting Children:** Designed for Families (Ages 6-12) and general learners.
2. **Parental Controls:** Parent Dashboard is locked behind a PBKDF2 salted 4-digit PIN.
3. **Advertising:** **Zero 3rd-party behavioral advertising or ad SDKs**.
4. **Child Safety Layer:** Automated content moderation with character-voiced safe redirects prevents exposure to inappropriate or harmful content.
5. **No Personal Information Requested:** Companions never solicit names, school names, phone numbers, addresses, or photos.
